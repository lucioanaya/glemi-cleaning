Customer Satisfaction, Quality Assurance & Complaints Policy

Our Commitment

At GLEMI, we are committed to providing professional, reliable, and high-quality cleaning services.

We believe in honest communication, clear expectations, and fair solutions.

Our Quality Assurance Policy is designed to address legitimate service concerns while ensuring that all requests are reviewed fairly and in accordance with the agreed scope of work.

1. Reporting a Cleaning Quality Concern

Customers are encouraged to report cleaning-quality concerns within 24 hours of service completion, whenever reasonably possible.

To submit a concern, please contact:

Email: glemiservices@gmail.com
Phone: +1 (236) 998-6275

Please provide:

  • Your name and booking reference, if available.
  • The date and address of the cleaning service.
  • A description of the specific concern.
  • Clear photographs of the affected area, when reasonably available.

We aim to acknowledge complaints within one business day.

Concerns reported after 24 hours will still be considered. However, subsequent use, cleaning, or changes to the property may affect our ability to assess the original service.

The 24-hour period is a reporting guideline and does not eliminate any rights or remedies available under applicable law.

2. Our Quality Assurance Commitment

If GLEMI verifies that an agreed cleaning task was missed or performed below reasonable professional standards, we will assess an appropriate corrective solution.

Whenever reasonably practical, our preferred solution is a complimentary corrective visit.

Corrective visits are limited to the verified areas or tasks included in the original booking.

They do not include:

  • Additional cleaning services.
  • Tasks excluded from the original booking.
  • New cleaning requests.
  • Areas that became dirty after service completion.
  • Conditions requiring specialized treatment outside the agreed scope.

We aim to arrange approved corrective visits within three business days, subject to scheduling, property access, and operational availability.

Where a corrective visit is unsuitable, unavailable, or insufficient, we will consider alternative remedies, including an appropriate refund when justified or legally required.

3. Service Scope and Customer Expectations

GLEMI performs cleaning services according to the tasks, service type, and property information agreed upon at the time of booking.

A standard cleaning service does not automatically include deep cleaning, stain removal, restoration, or specialized cleaning.

Additional services must be requested and approved separately.

The final cleaning result may depend on:

  • The property’s existing condition.
  • The level of buildup or deterioration.
  • The time and services booked.
  • Safe access to the cleaning areas.
  • Material and surface limitations.

GLEMI does not guarantee the removal of permanent stains, discoloration, mold, mineral deposits, or damage that cannot reasonably be resolved through the booked cleaning service.

4. Complimentary Corrective Visits

When a complimentary corrective visit is approved, GLEMI will coordinate a reasonable appointment with the customer.

Customers are expected to provide reasonable access to the affected areas.

If a customer declines a reasonable corrective visit, GLEMI will review whether an alternative remedy is appropriate under the circumstances.

Declining a corrective visit does not automatically entitle a customer to a full refund.

GLEMI reserves the right to evaluate each request individually, subject to applicable consumer protection laws.

5. Refund Requests

Refunds are not automatically issued for dissatisfaction or subjective differences in cleaning preferences.

Each request will be evaluated based on:

  • The agreed scope of work.
  • The cleaning services actually performed.
  • The nature and extent of the reported concern.
  • Available supporting evidence.
  • Whether reasonable corrective action is possible.
  • Applicable legal obligations.

Where justified, a partial or full refund may be offered.

Any approved refund will be communicated to the customer and processed through an appropriate payment method.

Nothing in this policy limits a customer’s entitlement to a refund or other remedy required by law.

6. Property Damage and Missing Items

GLEMI takes reports of property damage or missing items seriously.

Customers should notify us as soon as reasonably possible after discovering a concern.

To support a fair investigation, GLEMI may request photographs, item descriptions, purchase information, or other relevant evidence.

GLEMI will review the available information, including any relevant service records.

Pre-existing damage, normal wear and tear, fragile materials, and manufacturer-related defects will be considered when determining responsibility.

GLEMI does not automatically accept responsibility for every incident reported after a cleaning appointment.

Where GLEMI is legally responsible, an appropriate remedy may include repair, replacement, reimbursement, or another legally appropriate resolution.

Reports of damage or missing items are not subject to the 24-hour cleaning-quality reporting guideline.

7. Documentation and Quality Verification

To support service quality and fair complaint resolution, GLEMI may maintain relevant booking records, service checklists, and authorized before-and-after photographs.

Photographs will only be taken where permitted and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

Documentation may be considered when reviewing concerns about service completion, property conditions, or reported damage.

All customer information will be handled with reasonable care and used only for legitimate business purposes, consistent with applicable privacy law.

8. Conditions Outside the Agreed Service

GLEMI is not responsible for achieving cleaning results beyond the services reasonably agreed upon.

Examples include:

  • Permanent stains or discoloration.
  • Pre-existing damage.
  • Deteriorated grout, caulking, or silicone.
  • Excessive buildup requiring additional treatment.
  • Unsafe or inaccessible areas.
  • Specialized cleaning not included in the booking.
  • Areas affected by subsequent use or third-party activity.

These limitations do not exclude responsibility where GLEMI has failed to exercise reasonable care or where liability is imposed by law.

9. Complaint Review and Resolution

Our resolution process follows four steps:

Step 1 — Submit Your Concern

Contact GLEMI with details of the issue.

Step 2 — Quality Review

We review the booking details, agreed scope, available records, and supporting information.

Step 3 — Proposed Resolution

Where corrective action is appropriate, we will discuss a reasonable solution with the customer.

Step 4 — Follow-Up

We will communicate the outcome and, where appropriate, confirm whether the concern has been resolved.

10. Respectful Communication

GLEMI is committed to professional, respectful, and courteous communication.

We expect the same standard from customers and team members.

Abusive, threatening, or discriminatory conduct will not be tolerated.

Where appropriate and lawful, GLEMI may discontinue nonessential communications or decline future bookings. This does not affect the customer’s existing legal rights.

11. Escalation

Customers who disagree with an initial resolution may request an additional review by GLEMI management.

Management will assess the relevant information and communicate the outcome.

Customers retain the right to pursue any remedies available under applicable law.

12. Applicable Law

This policy is governed by the applicable laws of British Columbia and Canada.

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts, or overrides any consumer rights, statutory protections, or remedies that cannot lawfully be waived.
